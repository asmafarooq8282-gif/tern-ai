How computer access works

Ultra asks

The first time a task needs your computer, Tern stops and shows a permission request.

You decide

Allow it or decline. If you decline, Tern keeps helping in the chat and builds artifacts as usual.

You stay in charge

Every action appears in the activity list as it happens. One button turns access off.
